Story

Every student on this campus has had the late-night version of this story — the long walk back through a building that looks unfamiliar with the lights half off, the moment you can't remember if a door locks automatically behind you. Trapped follows one student who stays a little too late, and the building that seems to notice.

It isn't about a monster. It's about a very ordinary place starting to feel like it's paying attention to you.
